The full app in the Kit Room look. Every team appears as its shirt, and the number on the back is the data: win chance on fixtures, goals on results, ratings in lineups, points in fantasy and in your league table. Nothing was removed. FPL only covers the Premier League. FootyRush covers all five top leagues, for free. You can pick a fantasy squad from all five, predict any match, and open any game for stats, ratings and lineups. Pro adds the deep player comparisons from your original Insights tab.

Kit Room changes

A new shirt with the number dead centre

The shirt is drawn from the back: a short sleeve, a curved collar, and the name across the shoulders. The number is centred both ways in the torso so it reads like a real squad number, and it shrinks for three-character values like ratings.

Shirt numbers carry the data everywhere

Upcoming fixtures show each side's win chance on its shirt, results show goals, the match centre lineup shows every player's rating, the fantasy pitch shows points, and the league table is a row of shirt backs with each person's total. The model always wears yellow.

Logo A, the Speed Ball

The app icon and header use the Speed Ball mark, with its single yellow speed line tying into the corner-flag accent used for upsets and Pro.

Pinstripe green ground, white cards

The app sits on a pinstriped pitch green like a kit fabric, with white cards for content so data stays easy to read. Club colours only appear on shirts, so no licensed badges are needed.

Everything carried over

Every game opens a match centre, starting with a preview

Every fixture and result has a Preview with the model's odds and reasons, form, head to head, players to watch and team news. Finished games add team stats, player ratings and both lineups, with each player's rating in the corner of their marker. Previews of finished games also show how the model's call turned out.

All five leagues are free

The league filter works on Matchday, Predict and Fantasy without a paywall. Coverage brings people in, so it shouldn't be the thing you charge for.

Fantasy across leagues, or just one

Multi-league mode lets you build one squad from all five leagues, with at least 3 leagues and at most 3 players per club. Single-league mode limits the squad to one competition. This is the clearest difference from FPL.

Draft leagues alongside the classic game

Fantasy now has a Draft section: a live snake draft where each player can only belong to one team in your league, with a pick clock and model projections for every player. It works across all 5 leagues or within one. FPL has no draft mode, which makes it a second reason for groups to switch.

Insights moves into Fantasy as Compare

Player comparison now sits where fantasy decisions happen, and goalkeepers get their own stats. Free users see points, goals and assists. Pro unlocks the full per-90 breakdown and league-adjusted numbers, so Mbappé and Kane can be compared fairly.

The model still plays in your league

The model still sits in every private league table as a player, and predictions still earn a +2 bonus for calling a result the model gave under 25%.

Live goal alerts and the Pulse tab

Still cut for now. They need paid live data and are FotMob's strongest feature.

    How the model works

    FootyRush v5: Dixon-Coles team ratings plus a stacking layer, backtested on 12,556 matches.

    From ratings to a prediction

    1. Team ratings. A Dixon-Coles model rates every team's attack and defence from goals, shots on target and expected goals, with a home-advantage term and a correction for low-scoring draws.
    2. Expected goals and scorelines. Those ratings give each side's expected goals and a full scoreline grid for the match.
    3. Stacking layer. A multinomial logistic regression combines the ratings with ClubElo, recent shot and goal form, pressing intensity, rest days and the player quality of each side's expected XI to give the final win, draw and loss chances.
    4. Consistent outputs. The scoreline grid is rescaled to match those final chances, so the likeliest score, over 2.5 and both-teams-to-score all agree. "Why the model thinks this" lists the factors pushing hardest toward each side.

    How it's checked

    Every one of 12,556 top-five-league matches from August 2019 to September 2026 was predicted using only data from before kick-off, then scored with the ranked probability score, which rewards confident correct calls and punishes confident wrong ones.

    v5 is 13.9% better than league base rates (basic Poisson: 12.0%). Bet365's pre-match odds are 15.1% better, so the model doesn't beat the bookmaker yet. Plenty was tested and left out because it didn't help on validation, including table position, fixture congestion, Understat xG form, Opta big-chance form and transfer spending.

    Player ratings

    Every player starts a match on 6.0. The rating moves with what they did, weighted by position: goals, assists and chances created; shots on target; tackles, interceptions and duels won; passes into the final third; and mistakes like errors leading to shots. Goalkeepers are rated mainly on goals prevented against the quality of shots faced. 8.0 or higher is a standout game.

    Comparing players

    Percentiles compare a player's per-90 numbers with regular starters across the top five leagues, so 90 means better than 90% of them. League adjustment scales attacking numbers by league strength, estimated from players who moved between leagues and how their output changed. The factors in this demo are illustrative.

    What's next

    Re-export weekly so form, Elo and expected XIs stay current. Add confirmed lineups an hour before kick-off (backtested at 14.2%). Keep closing the gap to the bookmaker.
